15 November 2015 Poetry

18:06

You cannot understand hate
Until you have loved
You cannot understand the below
Until you have been above

You cannot understand loss
Until you have had
You cannot long for sanity
Until you have gone mad

I feel the anguish and malice
Burning in me like the sun
Beneath the surface
Rage, anguish, I am undone
