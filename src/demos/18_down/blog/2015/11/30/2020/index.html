30 November 2015 Poetry

20:20

So this is what we become
An eternally listless goodbye
This is how we write the pages
Of an un-lived wasted life

You could read the story
Like a sad old love song
All my good intentions
Seem to have come out wrong

This is all I could question
Does the punishment fit the crime?
I'm sitting here in the same old place
And I'm just running out of time
