30 November 2015 Poetry

Leonard Cohen

I've become Leonard Cohen
Locked in his Chelsea hotel
Stuck with the memories
Yeah I remember you well

There could have been a point
To this pointless future
I'm sitting here in the dark
And can't sing hallelujah
