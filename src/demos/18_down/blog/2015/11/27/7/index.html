27 November 2015 Poetry

7

I'd say I'm sorry
If it would mean anything
I'd go back and save us
If there was a point to anything

What would I say
If I walked passed you in the street?
Would you even look my way
Or would you just stare at your feet?

I would just ask how you are
Did all your dreams come true?
I'd tell you that I'm lost
And I just fucking miss you
