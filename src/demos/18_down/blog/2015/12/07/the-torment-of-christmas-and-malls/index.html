7 December 2015 Poetry

The torment of Christmas and malls

I walked in the mall today And just wanted to stop Sit down in the middle of the floor Cross my arms into each other Close my eyes, just sit there Till the Christmas carols didn't play anymore I can smell the Hugo Boss I can hear the laughter Watch the lovers holding hands They all so oblivious One man's world has ended But maybe that was always the plan
