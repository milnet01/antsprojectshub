7 December 2015 Poetry

Boys don't cry

They say big boys shouldn’t cry

So we just do it where no one will see

It’s been two years to the day

Since you walked away from me

We all say we strong

We can handle what life might bring

They say time makes it easier

But time hasn’t let me forget a thing

I keep thinking there will be some miracle

Some way out of this endless expanse

Someone will come and save me

That somehow there is a single chance

But it never comes

The days just roll into years

I’m left here wondering

Just when I’ll disappear
