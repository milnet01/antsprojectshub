8 June 2015 Poetry

Misguided

I build ivory towers
Put people on top
They tear it down
Then what have I got?

I know no cure
But I know the cause
I got all these memories
And don't want them anymore

Are we made to suffer
Misguided and thrown away
The children of last years dreams
With nothing left to say

Are we wordless whispers
Forgotten in the dark
And all we can ever hear
Is the breaking of our hearts
