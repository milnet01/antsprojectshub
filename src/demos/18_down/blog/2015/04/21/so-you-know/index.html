21 April 2015 Poetry

So you know

Words are so hollow
Yet I remember them all
Yet with a single look
I never wanted anything more

Its all meaningless I see
But its something you should know
I was freezing my ass off outside
But didn't want to go

There's nothing I understand
But I knew what you thought
When you looked at me that way
Its true , I felt it too
And that's just something I needed to say

Just so you know
