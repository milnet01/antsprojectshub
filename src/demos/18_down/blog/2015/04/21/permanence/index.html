21 April 2015 Poetry

Permanence

There is never hope
Its just a lie
These are the pieces
Of my fucked up life

Its the nothing's
Of all that could have been
Wishing impossible things
Close those eyes, dream

Even the sunshine is cold
The stars themselves are fated
The permanence of eternity
Its just deaths for which we are created
