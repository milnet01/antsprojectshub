21 April 2015 Poetry

The clearing

There's a man standing in a clearing
Lamenting all that we was
The payment has been made
But he paid more than he lost

Memories line his pockets
The thoughts choke his breathe
He used to look for answers
Now he just looks for death

He stands between the trees
The whole sky above
But he's done dreaming
Believing, in love

His hair is all matted
Clothes rank from being unkept
No forgetfullness or respite
Just this life, spent
