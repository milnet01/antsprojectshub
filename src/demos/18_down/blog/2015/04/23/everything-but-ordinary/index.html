23 April 2015 Poetry

Everything but ordinary

Funny how
She thinks I dont see
But I heard what she said
And she's everything but ordinary

There are reasons
I can't seem to explain
I wish I could think of something else
But shits just not the same

As long as she knows
Maybe one day things will be ok
There are things I know
But right now I cannot say

I thought the same as she
And I think I still do
So I might be quiet
But still thinking of you

I don't know the paths
Or how or what or when
Shit doesn't make sense now
Maybe it will then

The silence is defining
But right now its how it should be
If what I heard once is true
Then it will be you that finds me
