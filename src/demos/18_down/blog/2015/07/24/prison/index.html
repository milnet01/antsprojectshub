24 July 2015 Poetry

Prison

Imprisoned inside my mind
Feelings I can't define
I'm always running
From what's inside

It's a constant remorse
An echo of a life gone by
The shadows of all that was
I can't open my eyes

I'm so afraid to see
Just what I have become
If I had to stop to look
I regret all that I have done

Im lost out in some desert
Drowning out at sea
Please, please...
God help me
