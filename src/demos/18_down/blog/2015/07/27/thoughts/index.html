27 July 2015 Poetry

Thoughts

I never stood where you stand
In a world full of colours
It's just a shade of grey to me

I never saw what you saw
Just broken promises again
Another pointless tragedy

I wish I was someone else
To whom it all made sense
Now I'm stuck in my apathy
