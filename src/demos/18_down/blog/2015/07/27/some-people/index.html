27 July 2015 Poetry

Some people

For some people
Forever is just a single day
All their beautiful promises
Are simply washed away

I don't know the reasons
I  don't know the why
I'm running out of seasons
All the "I love you's" are just another lie

All I ever wanted
Is another broken dream
Some day if there is something more
Just where the hell have I been

I still stand here waiting
For something I won't find
All the time I've wasted
Is the life I've left behind

Can't they keep their word
Are their souls worth so little
Are are bones just made of glass
Are our hearts just that brittle

I'm begging for forgiveness
I don't know what more I can do
It's tearing me apart
And now I leave it up to You
