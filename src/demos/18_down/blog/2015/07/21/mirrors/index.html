21 July 2015 Poetry

Mirrors

I must look in the mirror
Imagining things I'd rather not see
I could rage, or be silent
Regardless, its how it will be

I cannot change anything
There is nothing I can undo
Pushing needles into my eyes
I love you, I hate you
