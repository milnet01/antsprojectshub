15 October 2015 Poetry

07:19

It's marvelous how things
Often get misconstrued
In many of these words
I'm not talking about you

But take what you will
Think just what you please
After all, we all get to choose
what we want to believe

If you find absolution
By giving me the blame
Then by all means do
The end result is the same
