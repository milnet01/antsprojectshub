6 October 2015 Poetry

07:11

Every Key unlocks a door

Every hurt leaves me wanting more

Staring into our dying sun

Every hope shatters to the floor

Leaving me in pieces

With nowhere left to run

I don’t want to be that man

Ruined by a single woman

That left long ago

I don’t need this empty life

Broken promises

Where I am, I just don’t know
