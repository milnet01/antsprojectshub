29 May 2015 Poetry

Fuck it

I'll say nothing now
The writings on the wall
Was trying to "do something"
But guess there's no point anymore

So I'll walk, take my shit and go
Its a pity but guess I'll never know
I can blame no one but myself
Just needed a little more time...
Perhaps its better if you hate me
Then at least you'll be fine
