21 October 2025 Poetry

21 October 2025

I buried my love in an unmarked grave

I cut off pieces of myself to send you

I gave all I had and I can’t let it fade

I wished so much that you stayed

Its black and it’s dark and it’s cold

I played my hands and now I fold

It’s just shadows where promises used to be

Why ok fuck why did you this to me?

You said forever and it was a lie

Now all I do every day is die

And i could ask a million times

And I’d still never understand why

It’s a broken heart and filth and bile

I crawled through the ash and bled for days

It’s a damn downright denial

And you broke me in so many ways

Love doesn’t fade

It just burns….
