31 July 2025 Poetry

31 July 2025

The hardest thing i ever had to
Was try to forget you
I still never have a moment free
And i fear it will take an eternity
To find a place where I'm at peace,
Where the memory of you can cease.
But every path I take, every dawn,
Just reminds me of a love that's gone
