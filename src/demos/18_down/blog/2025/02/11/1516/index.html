11 February 2025 Poetry

11 February 2025

Lay your head against my heart
Can you feel all my bad dreams?
The dark has opened wide
And it's one never ending scream

I didn't see the tangle
But waited in the rain
While you saw flowers
I saw the decay

I stood still, I ran
I tried all I could have done
I've waited for the miracle
For one that didn't come
