12 May 2025 Poetry

12 May 2025

We were just two people
And we had to say goodbye
I knew all your faults
And you knew mine

Our time was gone
And you closed the door
And since that day
Im not the same anymore

I sit around and wait
Staring at an empty chair
I had all these dreams
But you are just not there
