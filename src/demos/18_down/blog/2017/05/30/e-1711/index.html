30 May 2017 Poetry

E 17:11

Words are simply words

Yet they breathed the universe into life

And of these words there are many

And still too few to explain what it is

Yet if you could hear the echoes of my heart

And how it rages into chaos when you walk in

You would know all too well

How you are a dream made flesh
