Left here

You left me here<br>Just what must I do<br>Scraping on the inside<br>Until it pushes through<br><br>It's keeping me awake<br>Reaching through the scars<br>Burning with an endless ache<br>Like the spaces between the stars<br><br>The pieces fall<br>In places they all stay<br>My heart turns to ash<br>A beautiful lonely grey
