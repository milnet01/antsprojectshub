23 July 2017 Poetry

23 July 2017

Finally the world is asleep<br>My eyes are still open<br>Allowed to grieve for a moment<br>For all that I've broken<br><br>The memories that scratch <br>Through my damaged head<br>Take me into the ashes <br>And forgive me for what I've said<br><br>The lights are burning<br>Casting shadows against the wall<br>I can stop and maybe I can stop<br>Breathing till I don't anymore<br><br>Take me into the places<br>We never wanted to see<br>Take me into the fire<br>And forget about me<br><br>Free me from these thoughts<br>Crashing like waves against your shore<br>Make me hate rather than love<br>I don't know what that is anymore<br><br>Please<br>Please<br>Please <br>Please <br>
