4 January 2016 Poetry

21:15

What confessions could we make
To save us from our sins?
We lost it all before we began
And sacrificed our innocence

What could be possibly say
Anything more than we were told
We never find any peace
Have we all lost our souls?

The devil follows us home
The angels seem to stay away
We sleep between the lies
Trying to find the words to say
