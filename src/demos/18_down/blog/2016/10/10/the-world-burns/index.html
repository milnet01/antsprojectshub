10 October 2016 Poetry

The world burns

We've bought all that's for sale

We've been taught by marketing

We've been sold the latest dream

And all the while the world is burning
We drive the fanciest cars

Just so our friends can see

We feel we have the right to breed

When there are already 7 billion human beings
Our rights will lead us to extinction

We'll sell our souls for a single dollar

Our freedom is sold to the highest bidder

And our dreams are a diamond studded collar
