11 October 2016 Poetry

Not ok

Yeah let's do that, I'm bored 

Buy a new Mercedes Benz

Let's fuck, get married, divorced

After all we're just a means to an end
Let's choke on our humanity 

Our liberal little ways

Let's just allow everything

No it's not fucking ok 
Let's do something

Let's just post on our facebook wall

But the part we don't get

Just talking doesn't solve anything at all
