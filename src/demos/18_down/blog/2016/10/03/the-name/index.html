3 October 2016 Poetry

The name

I know that I'm hopeless

Since the day you walked away

I know I'm a lost cause

You had no reason to stay
Yeah Kathleen, I been drifting

That's the first time I could say your name

I got more ghosts than a cemetery 

You just the one that won't go away
Since the day you dissapeared

There's not a line I haven't crossed

I've woken in many beds

To try forget what I lost
