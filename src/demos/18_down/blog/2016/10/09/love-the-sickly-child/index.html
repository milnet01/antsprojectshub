9 October 2016 Poetry

Love, the sickly child

Love, my sickly child

Who ran away from home 

Love, my empty smile

Who broke my every bone
I cannot repeat the past

Looking for you in ever corner

Love, you do not wish to be found

And so, I shall simply mourn her
