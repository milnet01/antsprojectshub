15 May 2016 Poetry

Did you hear?

Did you hear the world is ending

People are still outside going to work

A chord plays on an old piano

And someone tries to forget they hurt

Did you hear the sky is burning

And the children still smile in the park

All the broken pieces lie on the floor

Of someones lonely heart

Did you hear that life is wasted

On meaningless empires of dirt

Measured in material wealth

We determine our own self worth

Yes, the world has ended....
