11 May 2016 Poetry

21:16

There is a loneliness so great

You can taste it in the very air

A dark misery so deep

That it's no longer called despair

That the very existence of man

Is an unyielding catatonic state

The questioning of eternity

A hysteria one cannot sedate

The miserable black day

The cold merciless night

A hopeless burden of solitude

With no happy end in sight
