11 August 2016 Poetry

All the hells and worlds

My anguish is a bottomless cup

So I shall pour and pour

I will lay in the dark and fester

Until I don't feel anymore
My avarice is an endless abyss

All the darkness of a life un-lived

Of all the hells and worlds

This loss I never believed
