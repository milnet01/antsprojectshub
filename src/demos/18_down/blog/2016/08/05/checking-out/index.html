5 August 2016 Poetry

Checking out

Life will come again

But for now it's all undone

The ashes of time wash away

We have nowhere left to run
Tired of letting go

Tired of starting again

I know of the past

Tired of asking when
They say the darkest hour

Is just before the dawn

Yet there seems to be no end

To this relentless storm
