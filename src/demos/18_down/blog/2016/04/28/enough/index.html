28 April 2016 Poetry

Enough

I avoid that which causes me pain

So don't look at me, or say my name

Life is that empty for me

I'd grab anything to fill the space

But I will not be mislead

By your pretty face

I'm tired of all the baggage 

I have enough of my own

I don't need the misery

So just leave me alone

I'm sick of the bullshit

The hidden agendas you try to sell

It's not good intentions I know

It's just another way to hell

It makes me so fucking mad

That I feel nothing but empty 

But I won't just settle for something

Because it's better than nothing

This one wants a fling 

This one wants a dad for her kid

This one wants me to pay her bills

But I don't want any of it

Another wants an accessory 

Another just to pass the time 

No matter the amount of justification

Some things just aren't right 

You made your choices

And I made mine

You live with them

And I'll do my time

There may be no happy ending

But I'm not going to play a game

So forget about what you think 

And please, go forget my name
