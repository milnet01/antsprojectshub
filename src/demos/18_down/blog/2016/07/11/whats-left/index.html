11 July 2016 Poetry

What's left

In the time that's passed

It would seem that love 

Is as foreign to me as mandarin

I apologize if I'm not easily swayed

But love is more than just mundane

And if that's the case, I won't fit in
You'll need to forgive me

But love needs to be extraordinary
