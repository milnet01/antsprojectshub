18 July 2016 Poetry

The gutters

Our undying love ends in the gutters

In paperwork and courts

Our promises of forever

Just end up in divorce

The futures we build

Lie in ashes and ruins

The dreams and promises

Simply broken unions

And what we are

Is less than what we were

Afflicted, diseased

There never was a cure
