5 December 2016 Poetry

The passage

The twisted fingers of reality 
My perception is warped and bent

Wandering the empty Sunday streets

Can't afford the emotional rent

My phone rings in my pocket

Someone I just used to know

I haven't heard their voice in years 

Yet they simply refuse to go

I sit at the window

Th coffee is getting cold

The children are laughing outside

And I'm getting old 

I thought I had time to write 

But now I'm simply middle age

I don't want to read anymore

Just turn the fucking page 

There's a bird on the wire 

It too will simply fly away

I'll fill my empire with dust 

And soon too I'll decay
