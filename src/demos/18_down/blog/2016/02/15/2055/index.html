15 February 2016 Poetry

20:55

Two black entities
The path lays between
Both converge in flames
Both end in misery

No matter which road
No matter the way I choose
I was made to carry this
I was born to lose

You can say as you please
Every promise turns into a lie
I don't feel anything anymore
I don't even care why
