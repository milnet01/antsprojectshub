28 March 2016 Poetry

Rinse And Repeat

I hope that you ache like I ache
a dull miserable pain
lingering in the door

We don't wait for happiness here
it never did come
so we will not wait anymore

the undeath of a man
a limbo of perpetual grief
breathing yet expired

wake up, eat, sleep
rinse, fucking repeat
and still we are tired
