6 March 2016 Poetry

17:20

My antagonistic queen
My mistress of misery
What else could you want
When you've already taken it all from me?

I decline with the setting sun
fading on some distant shore
I've bled out for nothing
I don't want this anymore

where are the promises?
just what direction should I take
just what is the truth
other than it's my heart that will break
