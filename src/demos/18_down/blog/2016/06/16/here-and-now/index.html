16 June 2016 Poetry

Here and now

Here and now

In the weeping arms of the day

In the dreadful silence around me

I can feel it all slipping away
You think you know me

Behind this damaged facade

The cage that holds my sins

Waiting to be dealt a final card
I know what it all means

Honestly, I know all to well

That there is no life here

That all this is a taste of hell
