19 June 2016 Poetry

How Long?

How long will it be?
Where it feels like we were never here
Could we have held on
Rather than to let it disappear?

The clouds have gathered
but the storm never did come
I sat waiting for it though
Wondering what I could have done

I broke rather than bend
here I am all over again
I'm waiting for the sun to shine
but these days just won't end

I don't have time anymore
to sit thinking about you walking out the door..
