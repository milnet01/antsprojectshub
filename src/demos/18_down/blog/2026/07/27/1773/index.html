27 July 2026 Poetry

27 July 2026

This wretched book of my life

The pages that seem to be endless repitition

All the questions, dead dreams

Where is my salvation?

Where are the hopes?

A single measure of purpose?

Where is the meaning?

Is it all just hopeless?
