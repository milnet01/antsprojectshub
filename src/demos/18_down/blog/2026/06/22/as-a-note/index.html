22 June 2026 Poetry

As a note…

Feel free to reach out, I’d love to know what you think, where you from and if this music made you feel anything.

This page is pretty messy, but it’s been bits and pieces over the years and I got to be honest, the idea of someone half way around the world in some place I’ve never been or perhaps never even heard of, listening to a song I recorded and played in my bedroom as a teenager, it’s fascinating to me.

I’d appreciate if you liked or subscribed or followed on any streaming service you might use. That’s the purpose of art, to make someone feel something and that would make all of this worth it, if you did feel something.

The songs are all remasters of original recordings, I played all guitars, bass, flute, piano and the vocals. The rest was done on midi keyboards and Cubase vst or GarageBand etc. It’s just been remastered and cleaned up to a professional point. Some of the lyrics are really cringe, but I wrote a lot as a confused teenager, that’s my excuse and I’m sticking to it, and I left it as it was for authenticity.

Well, go listen and let me know where you from….
