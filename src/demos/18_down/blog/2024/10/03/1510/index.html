3 October 2024 Poetry

3 October 2024

It's just the devil
Digging in the grooves
Pulling at the scars
A needle in the wound
It's just pain, it's just a dream
I'm not alright, I'm not ok
I'm anemic, cathartic
I want to be put away
Dragging the coffin nails
Draw the lines upon my skin
Is there any forgiveness
Or just punishment for sin?
There's no happy endings
Turning my head to the sky
The memories of things long gone
And the memories will not die
I want to go home
But does it even exist?
It's just anguish tearing through
Why live like this?
