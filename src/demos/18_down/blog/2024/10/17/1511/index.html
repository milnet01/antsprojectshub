17 October 2024 Poetry

17 October 2024

Love, is in fact servitude
The loss of self, chains
A miilstone around ones neck
It is nothing more
Than indentured slavery
But if it would be love
Why would i desire to be free?
I long for the shackles
Bound to another for eternity
Find me, take me, enslave me
