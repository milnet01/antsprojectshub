8 December 2014 Poetry

Meaning

Some people find purpose
In money, a title, a degree
Fancy cars, a bigger house
But none of that's for me

The world has changed
I'm trying to not be alarmed
But it seems the only place I find meaning
Is in a lovers arms

So it makes me a loser
A sentimental tiresome sap
But truth be told my dear
I really don't give a crap
