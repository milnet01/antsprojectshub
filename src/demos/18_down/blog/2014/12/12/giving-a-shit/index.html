12 December 2014 Poetry

Giving a shit

I  finding it harder and harder
Just to give a shit
Breathe in, breathe out
What's living got to do with any of it

I see the same places
Same stupid old grins
I don't care about politics
Or who loses, or who wins

Date after date
Another mundane conversation
The same pointless rantings
Feels like universal discrimination

Is it that wrong to believe in love
To think that it actually exists
It's hard to fucking hold hands
When you're busy clenching your fists

Why should I trust someone
When it's all just misrepresentation
It's a joke, the world as it is
And all I feel is alienation
