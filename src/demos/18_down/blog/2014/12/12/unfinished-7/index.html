12 December 2014 Poetry

Unfinished 7

I don't want to change the world
Don't want to change anyone else
I can't change the past
And I won't change myself

The world can burn for all I care
Each will answer for their own
Some so self righteous it makes me sick
And some don't bother to atone

I'm a sinner, a liar, a thief
A broken man stricken with grief
