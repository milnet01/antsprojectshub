8 July 2014 Poetry

You turned

You turned away
And let my heart turn to ash
The clouds were gathering
To greet my great collapse

I never was much
Didn't feel I had anything to prove
Love like hope is gone
And I have nothing left to lose

But I know what it takes to love
Till it feels like my soul is in shreds
Like I could never pick up the pieces
But I have nothing instead
