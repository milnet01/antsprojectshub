18 July 2014 Poetry

It's all good

Promises we make, promises we break
I stood blinking in the light
Words coming I didn't want to hear
And somehow it will be alright

So hold be now
And my heart can be buried in the dust
Stay, don't every go
I'm dying so to trust

Let me stand next to you
Let me hope in the darkness
I have forgotten all my ghosts
Let us always stay like this

All I'd say if she ever was
If something ever came to be
Walking through the cracks in my heart
In some way she could love me

It's all that I knew
And I close my eyes to change the view
Give me something that I can believe
Find me, because I'm missing you
