20 July 2014 Poetry

Noise

Close your heart like a closet
The doors shouldn't be open anyway
This is the reckoning
And I'll push everyone away

The noise makes this all feel
Like I'm going home
If the beatings are familiar
Best you know you not alone

The world is lost to me
Be still and know
I heard that many times before
I have no choice cos I got nowhere to go

It's not sad, it's not a problem
I'll just crawl up on the floor
I'll lie here and wait
And when I'm done, I'll wait some more

I'll carry on
Another day, I'll still pray
I'll lie here in bed and hope
Until the noise goes away
