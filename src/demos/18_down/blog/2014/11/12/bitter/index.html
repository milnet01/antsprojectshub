12 November 2014 Poetry

Bitter

I'm not bitter
Who's bitter? Just tired of the mass delusion
No wonder we cannot find answers
Since its all just politically correct confusion
Lets twist the scriptures
Lie in the churches
We're enlightenment now
So who needs the actual verses
Let's give prisoners rights
When they violate our own
It's inhuman to cage them
Even if they murder people at home
Lets have freedom of speech
When we can't say what we think
Lets smile at each others faces
When we can't stand the stink
Lets plead insanity or label our shortcomings
Surely it's the right thing to do
Lets medicate our poor humanity
You know what, I'm over it, fuck you
