15 November 2014 Poetry

For you

I waited so long to hear your name
And i still smile as I think of it
I cherish the thought of your laughter
And your oh so clever wit
Now that it's all over
And the last slivers of dusk go by
My bags are packed
Just please, wave me goodbye
I'm just a re-run of the same old show
Everything shall pass, we all need to go
And I'll send you postcards to remind you
"I love you", just so you know
