11 November 2014 Poetry

Random

I hear the voices
Mixed with other sounds
I don't know where I am
But I got to get out of this town

I'm wondering around
In some strange city
I don't know where I'm going
But this is no place to be

It's overwhelming
It's just too loud
I'm tired of feeling alone
But I can't be in a crowd
