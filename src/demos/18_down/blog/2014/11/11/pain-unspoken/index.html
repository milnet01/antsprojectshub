11 November 2014 Poetry

Pain unspoken

I wish I had better words
Rather than scratching at the walls
But silent I cannot be
I'll write the words in tears and blood
I'll keep screaming till my lungs give out
Just look at what you fucking done to me

You can keep looking
Through the broken windows
But you'll never get a piece of me
I'll lie between the unwritten pages
Picking at these weeping wounds
Safeguarding my perfect tragedy

You lied, time and time again
You broke me, and I'm stronger than most men
You turned me into this wretched thing
You took my dreams, my hopes, my love
I gave you more, but it was never enough
I surrendered my heart, in return I have nothing

I sit on this rotten throne
Crawling on my knees in this empty home
Pleading for some sanity
Take away this fucking memory
I'm begging, screaming
There's nothing left of me
