1 November 2014 Poetry

A single moment

I can recognise the moment inside me
It's never been easy looking at all of you
A life turning on a single point in space
I just don't know what I'm supposed to do

All the faces, smiles, thoughts
Places and things I'll never be a part of
Looking in the empty spaces between the stars
For a single thing of value, love

Maybe we can find a common ground
Perhaps share the same point of view
Between the lies and hidden  truths
Give in, let me find you
