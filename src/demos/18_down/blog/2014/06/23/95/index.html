23 June 2014 Poetry

23 June 2014

Some say we need faith Some say love is the key Some say the answers are there But there are questions for me I feel the hunger Leading me astray I need a direction Since I've lost my way It's burning inside me Like 1000 blazing sun's It's tearing me apart And I'm coming undone I'm tired of waking in the same old place The black sheets around my body I dream of the same old things That you still loved me I'm tired of driving to work I hate this, the lack of a witness It feels unfinished And now I lack a purpose
