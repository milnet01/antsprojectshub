23 June 2014 Poetry

23 June 2014

I want to see the shape of your neck
Each line of your perfect smile
I want to drown in your ocean
And see eternity in your eyes

I want to be covered in night
And be reborn with each day
I want to love like I'm about to die
And be lost in the words you say
