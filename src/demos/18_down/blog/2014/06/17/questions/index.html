17 June 2014 Poetry

Questions

I searched for answers
While you stare at the sea
You're holding hands
I'm wondering why you did this to me

I was beating myself
And you were loving some guy
You have all you want
And I'm fucking asking why

Was I not good enough
Was I all the things you said
For the love of all that was good
Just get the fuck out of my head

A year ago I was standing where he stood
All of your lies and bullshit and I wanted to see it through
Every answer is another question
And I haven't got a fucking clue
