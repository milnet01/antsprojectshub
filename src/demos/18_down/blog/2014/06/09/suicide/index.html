9 June 2014 Poetry

Suicide

I feel like a voodoo doll
Posing for a suicide
Torn up and full of pins
Yeah, I figured, you lied

I feel like a burn victim
I'm in so much pain
I'm screaming for mercy
But you burn me again

It's a fucked up life
The choices still have an effect
The paths all going nowhere
I was lost, last time I checked

I found the blades
Buried beneath my skin
All your words still linger
Like my eternal sin
