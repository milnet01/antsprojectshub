21 September 2014

I planned to go back to church today
But the doors were closed
Felt like I knocked on the doors of all I knew
And yet nobody was home

If I could find the words
Yet so many there seem to be
I'm so very lost my friend
I'm so very broken you see

I wanted to do it right this time
Make everything just make sense
I couldn't see the forest for the trees
And got lost in my own pretense
