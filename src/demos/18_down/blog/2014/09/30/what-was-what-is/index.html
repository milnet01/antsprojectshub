30 September 2014 Uncategorised

What was, what is

Replaying a then
Won't change the now
Finding a why
Won't make another how

Unpredictable outcomes
Are outcomes nonetheless
Hope turns to loss
Love turns to bitterness

Imagining what is
Reliving the past
Pictures of a possible present
Could not make anything last

What is shall be
What was is no more
No thought, understanding or reason
Could return what was before
