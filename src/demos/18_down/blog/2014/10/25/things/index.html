25 October 2014 Uncategorised

Things

I've been drinking the poison
I feel like I'm immune
I feel diluted and weak
Sitting in this empty room

It makes no difference
Pessimistic or optimistic
Same results ensue
It makes no sense
Just makes me sick
Thinking of you

Time passes by
The pendulum swings
A lifetime of forgotten things
