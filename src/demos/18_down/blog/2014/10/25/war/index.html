25 October 2014 Poetry

War

It's none of your f*cking business
I don't care i want to forget
I got nowhere left to go
So leave me to drown in regret
I've been fed on raised on sh*t
For so many endless years
I don't care if you think I'm weak
You weren't f*cking living here
So I'll site here in the corner
Just for a little longer this time
Cursing the world under my breath
Forget it, I'll be fine
It's my sentence, it's my disease
Crawling around on my hands and knees
So you hear me? Do you understand?
Don't waste my f*cking time please
Cut me up, feed me to the pigs
Maybe you can win this war
But before you raise your fists
Know what the hell you're fighting for
