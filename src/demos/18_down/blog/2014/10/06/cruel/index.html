6 October 2014 Uncategorised

Cruel

How cruel to make me love you
So terribly wicked to make me care
Stripping the dreams from my head
Taking me away then leaving me there

How wretched the kisses you gave
How sweet the poison in your eyes
The venom dripping from your lips
I willingly went to my demise

How awful the emptiness I have
When nothing should let this heal
How dreadful the days are to me now
When the anguish is all I feel
