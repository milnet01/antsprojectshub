27 October 2014 Poetry

Half to my one half

You'll never have my memories
Or all the things I've seen
Never know the places and heartache
Or share this forgotten dream

I'd give up my entire world
To stop my heart from being filled with sand
The ashes of the love I once felt
Now bitterness makes me what I am

I recognise the lines
See the shape of that beautiful face
The darkness at two in the morning
Now I'd rather just be some other place

The last thing I ever wanted to do
Was say the word goodbye
I could feel our love dying
But there was no point still to try

I'd still surrender my life
To hear your voice one more time
I can see it still as if it were today
But now only the emptiness is mine

I was born with a hole in my heart
That knowledge, power or things cannot fill
I'm looking for a half to my one half
And I am looking still
