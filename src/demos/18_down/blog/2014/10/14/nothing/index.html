14 October 2014 Uncategorised

Nothing

Seems to be a strange sensation
Not angry or sad, but a type of hollow
Like a vacuum or a void
It's not tiring, or anything described
Everything has a tint of nothing to it

It's as if space itself has turned inside out
Looking from the other side of a glass wall
I can see everything, smell the world
Taste the food, niether hungry or sated
It's just another step in the equation

To what answer will still need to be determined
The end is the same as the beginning
Two ends of a piece of string
Both looking the same depending on the angle
I just see the middle, not caring either way
