26 October 2014 Poetry

It's not easy

Now there's nothing left to lose
There's nothing left to say
Who took who for granted
And brought on the decay
It's never an easy thing
To keep to the choices we make
Keep breathing, hoping
That this was not a mistake
It's not easy here
Having to live in this way
The same old things
The same old day
