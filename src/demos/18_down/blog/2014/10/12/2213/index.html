12 October 2014 Uncategorised

22:13

I let it fall
I let down my guard
Things put back together
I stood watching the flames
Still they smoulder in the ash
They will burn forever

The lines, the shape
The wretched twist of fate
Writhing in this tattered soul
All the drugs in the universe
All the medicated peace
Could not make this whole
