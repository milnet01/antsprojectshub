1 March 2018 Poetry

1 March 2018

We all go to heaven

Thieves and liars all they say

We don't ask any questions

We just lie down and pay

False piety amidst the scraps

On our hands and knees it never stops

When they take all that you dream

What else have you got

Put your hands up

Choke on your rotten lies

Preach while you steal

It's the truth you despise
