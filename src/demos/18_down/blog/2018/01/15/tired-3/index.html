15 January 2018 Poetry

Tired

I'm tired

Dreadfully weary of it all

The same smoked out afternoons

Coffee stained mornings

I'm sick of breathing

Seeing houses smiling like corpses

Doors like grim teeth in the dusk

Waking sleeping waking sleeping

I'm over the politics and lies

The promises and dead dreams

The lack of hope, the lack of everything

Of tomorrow's that never come
