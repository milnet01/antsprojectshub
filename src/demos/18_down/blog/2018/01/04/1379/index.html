4 January 2018 Poetry

4 January 2018

I know all the pieces fit

I devoured the sum of its parts

Revealed to be entwined with fear

Entropy lack of compassion

Decomposition of all hope

Overwhelmed until we disappear

Holding us in fleshy cages

Reality twisted to one single point

Forget the truth, we are here
