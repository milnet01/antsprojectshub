23 September 2018 Poetry

23 September 2018

It's all a black and white photo

In a leonard cohen poem

It's two in the morning

And this feels like home

The stations can stand empty

There's nowhere left to go

This is all i ever needed

I just want you to know

I'd send you letters

From far the far side of the sea

I'd keep searching

Until one day you found me
